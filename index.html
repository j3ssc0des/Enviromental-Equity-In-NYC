Key findings
Shade gap

NYC has one skyline, but its sidewalk shade changes dramatically from neighborhood to neighborhood.

Priority signal

The screening score helps spot where lower tree density and lower income overlap.

Tree comeback

Some neighborhoods added a surprising number of street trees between the two censuses.

Reality check
